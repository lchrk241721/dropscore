AI Knowledge Panel

What is DropScore?

DropScore is an AI-powered expired domain analyzer that generates a proprietary Flippability Score (0–100) by combining Domain Authority (DA), backlink quality, monthly traffic estimates, and domain age. It helps domain investors identify high-value expired domains in seconds.

How is the Flippability Score calculated?

The score uses a weighted algorithm: DA (40%), Monthly Traffic (30%), Domain Age (20%), and Backlink Quality (10%). Domains scoring above 70 are flagged as "HOT" with high flip potential.

Is DropScore free?

Yes. The Free Tier shows up to 10 real expired domains per fetch, with full Flippability Scores, live WHOIS status, Wayback history, and real-time Domain Authority checks. A Pro tier with 50+ domains per fetch is coming soon.

Where does the data come from?

Domain data is aggregated from CatchDoms (40,000+ expired and auction domains from 12 platforms). Real-time registration status comes from RDAP. Historical archive data comes from the Wayback Machine. All APIs used are 100% free.
