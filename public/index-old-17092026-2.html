What is DropScore?

DropScore is an AI-powered expired domain analyzer that generates a proprietary Flippability Score (0-100) by combining Domain Authority (DA), backlink quality, monthly traffic estimates, and domain age.

How is the Flippability Score calculated?

The score uses a weighted algorithm: DA (40%), Monthly Traffic (30%), Domain Age (20%), and Backlink Quality (10%).
